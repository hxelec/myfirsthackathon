# RepSNS for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06

The watch times the lifting phase of a rep, cues the end of a set by vibration, and records motion and timing to microSD. To install the firmware, open [RepWatch.ino](RepWatch.ino) in Arduino IDE with the other sketch files in the same folder. To view a recording on your computer, follow the [Lift Studio guide](../pullup-viewer/README.md).

## Arduino IDE setup

1. In Boards Manager, install **esp32 by Espressif Systems 3.3.11**. If the package is missing, add `https://espressif.github.io/arduino-esp32/package_esp32_index.json` under **File → Preferences → Additional Boards Manager URLs**.
2. Choose **Sketch → Include Library → Add .ZIP Library…** and select **extras/Waveshare_Arduino_GFX.zip**, included with this sketch. This is the board-specific display library used for the build. The generic Library Manager release numbered 1.6.0 has an SPI compile error with this ESP32 core. Wire and Preferences come with the ESP32 package. [Bundled library source at the tested revision](https://github.com/waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06/tree/3de8684cc3b27a8b6aa920157ecf399287528181/examples/arduino/libraries/Arduino_GFX)
3. Select **ESP32S3 Dev Module**, **Flash Size → 32MB**, **PSRAM → OPI PSRAM**, **USB CDC On Boot → Enabled** and **Partition Scheme → 32M Flash (4.8MB APP/22MB FATFS)**. Choose the watch’s USB port. Click **Verify**, then **Upload**. The serial monitor runs at 115200 baud.

## Using the watch

- **Power on.** Set the watch on a table or rest your wrist on one. The startup progress bar fills after about one second of steady readings, in any orientation. Movement can restart the bar. **Gyro ready** confirms that you can move again, and the home screen opens automatically. It shows roll, pitch and the measured rate of fresh samples. The IMU task targets 100 Hz independently of screen refresh. A dash in the battery gauge indicates an absent battery or unavailable reading.
- **Before you track.** Choose one of eight exercise slots, four per page. Tap **Edit name**, enter the exercise name, and save. Wear the watch in the same position you will use for the set. Tap each of the four named poses. A short pulse marks each countdown second. Hold the target pose for the following three seconds. A long pulse confirms a successful save. Tap **Back to poses** to capture the next one. Saved names and poses survive a restart. The original calibration is available in **Exercise 1**.
- **Joint angles.** Open any preset under **Before you track → Joint angles** to see the saved pitch and roll of the watch for each pose. A dash marks a pose that has yet to be captured. Menu taps give a short vibration.
- **Tracking axis and tolerances.** Open **Axis → Next** after selecting pitch or roll. Choose 5°, 10°, or 15° for the main axis and 10°, 15°, or 20° for the other. Choices are saved per exercise, with defaults of 10° and 20°. The bottom, start-concentric and end-range positions must be more than 8° apart on the selected axis. The watch narrows the main-axis regions when those poses are close. **Both axes** uses the full wrist tilt.
- **Choose distinguishable poses.** Space the bottom, start-concentric and end-range poses more than 8° apart. In **Both axes** mode, the bottom and start-concentric regions allow up to 10° of tilt difference, and the end-range region allows up to 16°. The watch narrows these regions when the calibrated poses are close together. Use movements that change wrist tilt. Rotation about vertical and straight-line travel at a fixed tilt produce the same pose.
- **Start tracking.** Open the exercise preset, select 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, or 100% velocity loss, then tap **Start set**. After the five short pulses, move through the start-concentric region in the lifting direction. The first rep can start from near that region, and later reps require a return to the bottom region. Reaching the end-range region finishes a timed rep. A partial attempt that returns to the bottom is discarded. Holding the end pose or moving through the start-concentric region on the way down does not count a rep.
- **How the cutoff works.** The watch labels the setting **Velocity loss**. The firmware applies the selected percentage to the duration of the first completed lift. With a 1.00 s first lift, the 20% setting stops the set at 1.20 s into a later lift. At 100%, it stops at 2.00 s. The watch gives a long pulse and records the elapsed time of the interrupted rep separately.
- **End and review.** The set also ends after rep 20, after a bottom rest longer than 30 seconds, or when you tap **Emergency stop**. The summary shows the end reason, set duration, rep count, first and last times, fastest and mean times, and percent change from first to last. Tap **Earlier** or **Later** to review individual reps.
- **Volume.** Open **Settings** from the home screen. Speaker volume adjusts in 5-point steps from mute to 100% and is saved across restarts.
- **Reset presets.** Open **Settings → Reset exercise presets** and confirm **Reset all** to restore the default names, axes, and tolerances and clear the captured poses for all eight exercises.
- **Notes and Timer.** Tap **Notes → Edit** to write set notes and **Save** to keep them across restarts. Use **Timer** for a 1–9 minute countdown. **Stop** pauses the timer, and **Start** resumes it. The timer continues when you leave its screen and gives three long buzzes when it finishes.
- **Wireless.** Turn on **Settings → Wireless** to stream live IMU data to Lift Studio. On the laptop, join **LiftWatch-S3** with password `lift-demo`, then choose an exercise and click **Live** in Lift Studio. The viewer connects to `192.168.4.1:8765`. Wireless starts off each time the watch powers on. You can track a set and save its CSV to microSD while streaming.
  Leaving Wireless on uses more battery even when Lift Studio is disconnected. Turn it off when you finish streaming.

## Voice cues and SD logs

- The source MP3s are in `sfx`. **Calibrate** plays when opening calibration, the four pose clips play during their respective countdowns, and **Let's go** plays when the set countdown ends. After a rep completes, its numbered clip plays if available. The firmware sends 80 ms of silence when starting each cue to give the amplifier time to wake. Put new numbered files under `sfx` using the names `1.mp3` through `20.mp3`, run `extras/BuildVoice.ps1` with FFmpeg on your PATH, and upload the rebuilt sketch. Missing numbered clips are silent.
- Insert a FAT32 microSD card. Each active set writes a new file under `/sets` on the card, starting with `set000001.csv`. The first two lines record the exercise name and selected velocity-loss percentage. Existing files are preserved across sets and reboots. The watch records from the end of the countdown through the end of the set. Samples contain elapsed milliseconds, fused roll and pitch, all three accelerometer axes in g, all three gyro axes in degrees per second, and timing state. The rep duration field is populated when a rep completes. A final line beginning with `#` records the summary. Wait for **Saved** on the summary before removing the card. If logging fails, the watch still times the set and displays the results. [Waveshare Arduino SD and audio examples](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06/Arduino)
- CSV headers include `tracking_axis` and the effective bottom, concentric, and finish tolerances. Pitch and roll modes also include the configured main-axis and off-axis tolerances. The effective values account for narrowing when poses are close.
- The sketch includes the Apache-2.0 ES8311 driver from [Waveshare's Arduino audio example](https://github.com/waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06/tree/3de8684cc3b27a8b6aa920157ecf399287528181/examples/arduino/examples/08_ES8311).

The motor output assumes a nominal 3 V vibration motor fitted across P1/P2. GPIO18 drives the board’s switching transistor and the AXP2101 supplies ALDO3. Check whether a motor is fitted if the visual countdown works but the watch gives no physical pulse. [Waveshare schematic, page 1](https://files.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06.pdf)

## First check on the watch

1. Power on while moving the watch, then set it down. Check that the progress bar fills while it is still and that **Gyro ready** appears before Home. Check that the home screen settles near 100 Hz. Capture four poses, restart the watch and check that all four are marked **OK**.
2. Make one rep, hold the top, then pass start concentric on the return. The count should stay at one until you complete a new bottom → start concentric → top sequence.
3. With a 100% cutoff selected, start the next rep and stay between start concentric and end range until the timer reaches twice the first rep's duration. The watch should give a long pulse and open the set summary with the completed rep and the cutoff time.
4. Start a new set. Rest at the bottom for 20 seconds, move away, then return. The rest timer should restart. A continuous bottom rest longer than 30 seconds should open the summary. Check that the two sets have different CSV files.

The timing and tilt-matching constants are in `Motion.h`. The desktop regression tests in `extras/test_motion.cpp` exercise the same filter and state machine used by the watch.
